Speech Arena uses one cookie, to keep you signed in. With your permission, it also saves a few things in your browser's local storage. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party cookies.

## Strictly necessary

These are needed for the service to work, so they don't need your consent.

| Name | Type | Purpose | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sa_session` | Cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict) | Keeps you signed in. Page scripts can't read it | Until you sign out, after 24 hours without activity, or at most 7 days |
| `sa.consent.v1` | Local storage | Remembers the choice you made below | Until you change it or clear your browser data |
| Security token | Page memory only | Protects your requests from forgery | Until you close the tab |

## Optional

These are only saved if you allow them.

| Name | Category | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sa.settings.v1` | Preferences | Theme, sidebar and default practice mode |
| `sa.analyses.v1:<account>` | History | Scores and flaws from your past analyses, so you can see your progress |
| `sa.battles.v1:<account>` | History | Results of your 1v1 battles |

If you don't allow history, your results are kept only until you close the tab. Recordings are never saved in your browser or on our server.

## Changing your choice

Use **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page, or Settings → Privacy. Turning a category off deletes what was saved for it in this browser. You can also clear site data in your browser's settings.

## Third parties

None. Fonts and all page files are served by Speech Arena itself.
