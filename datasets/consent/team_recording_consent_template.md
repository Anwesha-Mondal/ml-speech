# Speech Arena: Team Recording Consent Form

**Project**: Multimodal AI Hackathon 2026 (Track C) - Speech Arena
**Data Controller**: Speech Arena Team

## Overview
As a member of the Speech Arena team, you are voluntarily providing audio recordings of your voice reading specific transcripts. Some of these recordings will represent "ideal" deliveries, while others will be intentionally "flawed" (e.g., rushed pacing, monotone pitch) to train our contrastive speech analytics system.

## Consent Details
1. **Voluntary Participation**: I agree to record my voice for the Speech Arena dataset.
2. **Rights & Licensing**: I hereby assign the recordings to the public domain (CC0) or license them under CC BY 4.0, allowing the team to redistribute, modify (derivative works), and use them commercially as part of the dataset submission.
3. **Anonymization**: I understand that my recordings will be tagged with an anonymized `speaker_hash` in the public dataset, though my voice itself is inherently biometric.
4. **Data Retention**: I understand I can request deletion of my raw source audio from internal servers up until the point the dataset is officially frozen and published for the hackathon (deadline: Oct 15, 2026).
